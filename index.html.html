Customer Intake + Auto Scoring
Use when a customer contacts you from WhatsApp, Messenger, Viber, phone, or a form.
This is the first automation layer. Paste or type customer details, then the app classifies them as HOT, WARM, or COLD. Later, a backend webhook can send WhatsApp/Messenger/Viber messages into this same scoring system automatically.
HOT

Ready lead

Has intent, location, budget, timeline, and appointment/viewing signal.

WARM

Nurture lead

Interested but missing budget, urgency, or appointment commitment.

COLD

Low priority

General inquiry, no budget/timeline, or just browsing.
